# Manuscript addendum: evidence scope and current external evaluation

The available evidence supports a layered, implementation-specific account of resource-bounded inference. Source-level CBMC checks establish properties of selected deployed control functions under explicit integer-domain and abstraction assumptions. Binary and pinned-Ibex RTL analyses provide additional evidence for a particular firmware image and Simple System configuration. A separate DE0-CV run exercised a dynamic 64-pixel input path on ten preselected engineering-pilot images in two modes (20 requests); the saved request/response records were reported to match the frozen Q15 CPU oracle.

The pilot is a functional integration check, not a test of population accuracy, statistical noninferiority, or general speed superiority. No primary external-dataset measurements have been made in this work session. The 7,626-row file produced here is an offline schedule template only and must not be described as FPGA data. Existing preregistration called for three sessions; a proposed one-session exploratory amendment is separately saved and has not been approved or applied.

The evidence does not establish a physical WCET, hard deadline guarantee, or external USB-Blaster timing signoff. Data exposure history and writer clustering are unresolved, and preregistration timing is not independently anchored. Accordingly, any future external primary results would remain exploratory unless these eligibility limitations are resolved. Claims about cycle behavior must identify the exact board/build/session and distinguish Ibex simulation from FPGA measurements.
